Good to know

Common questions.

What areas do you serve?
We service all of Northeast Pennsylvania (NEPA), including Wilkes-Barre, Scranton, Kingston, Pittston, Clarks Summit, Dunmore, Hazleton, the Back Mountain, and surrounding areas. Don't see your town? Call us — chances are we can still help.
Do you bring your own supplies?
Yes. We bring everything we need — products, tools, and equipment. If you prefer we use your supplies (eco-friendly, fragrance-free, pet-safe), just let us know in advance and we'll happily accommodate.
Are you insured?
Absolutely. M&M Cleanse is fully insured and bonded. We'll happily provide proof of insurance for any commercial client or property manager who requests it.
How does pricing work?
Pricing depends on the size of your space, the type of clean, and how often we visit. Recurring clients get the best rates. Estimates are always free — request one above or call (570) 706-1413.
Do you handle Airbnb turnovers?
Yes — Airbnb and short-term rental turnovers are one of our specialties. We coordinate with same-day check-outs and check-ins, restock essentials, launder linens, and stage your space hotel-quality every time.
How do I book the Memorial Day Sale?
Easy — fill in the quick form above and check the "Memorial Day Sale" box, or call (570) 706-1413 and mention the offer. Spots fill quickly ahead of the long weekend, so don't wait.
